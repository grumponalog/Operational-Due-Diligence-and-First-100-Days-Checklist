What diligence should catch

A private equity partner retained CBS for operational due diligence on a commercial glass processor being carved out of a larger conglomerate. CBS modeled the stand-alone cost structure independently and identified $3.4 million in one-time and recurring costs the seller had not presented, which was used in the final price negotiation. The same review of operational performance surfaced $2.8 million in annualized savings, roughly 3 percent of sales.
